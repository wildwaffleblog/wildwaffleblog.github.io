### LUT pack

<div class="image-row">
  <img src="/img/LUT_pack_cover.png" alt="LUT usage cover">
</div>

A small pack of LUTs designed for practical use in real edits. Each LUT has a clear role. They're not overloaded or fragile. Just drop them in and move on with your cut.

BACKLOG - A gentle Apple Log to Rec.709 conversion that respects the original image.

DAILY - A confident everyday look with warm cinematic tones, deeper shadows and enough contrast. It doesn't scream "graded" but says you have a vision.

MUTE - Soft, restrained, and slightly detached. Colors are subdued and the black point is lifted, creating a hazy atmosphere. Quiet morning mood.

RETRO - Pure monochrome with attitude. Strong blacks and clean highlights. When color becomes a distraction, this brings the frame back.

STORYPOINT - Cooler tones, controlled contrast and a subtle cinematic distance. Quietly shapes the image into something more deliberate than plain Rec.709. Perfect for something narrative.
