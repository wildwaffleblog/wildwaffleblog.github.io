DUO is out - part two of the trilogy. I changed the plan for this video a few times. First I wanted to make a big trip in spring, then ride 100 kilometers closer to home. Neither of those happened. But instead, I ended up in Saint Petersburg and decided to ride 100 miles to Vyborg. The forecast looked easy, but in reality, it rained all day, there was strong wind, slippery roads and a lot of time to wonder why I was even doing this.

The strange part is that I had already written the whole Dragon lore before the trip - the road talking to you, trying to make you turn back. Somewhere in the middle of the ride, I actually started talking out loud to myself, the road and the wind. So yeah. The metaphor became too real for me. I made it to Vyborg. I defeated the Dragon I created for no real reason.

One last part left to film. And TRES was already going to be different...
