TRES is the final part of the trilogy, and this time I decided to drop the voice-over completely. I wrote the track myself and then processed it through an AI model, so the music became the narration. For me, the whole trilogy ended up being about something pretty simple: sometimes you have to stay home to find the biggest adventures. And sometimes the biggest adventures only matter because they bring you back home and make you see it differently.

A lot changed in my life this year. I moved. I changed a lot of plans for the future, my priorities, my lifestyle. I changed the main way I move around the city, and because of that I started seeing the place where I live differently too. I used to be genuinely annoyed that we live on a plain. I would always say: "Give me mountains!" Now I look at long flat streets and country roads and think: "Oh yeah!.. These are perfect for my road bike."

So I guess I learned to love this place in a different way. I'm going to live here for a long long time. And all my Dragons were probably in my head anyway. And if they start fucking with me again, I'll kill every single one of them.
